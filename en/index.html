A few words

About me

My path started in networks and web development, then moved towards software engineering and artificial intelligence for education. I did my bachelor's at the Faculty of Sciences of Tunis, then a research master's in software engineering at ISI, where my thesis was about a neuro-symbolic mathematics tutor for the Tunisian baccalaureate.

My PhD is about adaptive learning systems: how a system can choose the way it teaches when it knows the learner only partly, while staying reliable.

Alongside research, I teach at ISI as a part-time lecturer, and I keep one foot in software development, from networks to the web.
